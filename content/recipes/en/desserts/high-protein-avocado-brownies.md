I read online that you can make this recipe from only three ingredients (bananas, cacao, and peanut butter), but I had a ripe avocado and wanted to use it. I will maybe try with peanut butter as well, but avocado is healthier and has more volume (fewer calories) than peanut butter.

If you are a fan of chocolate, brownies, fudge, or cookies, this recipe is definitely for you. I made many variations of brownies in the past, but this is the best version of brownies I ever made. I used only a fork to make the mixture and the air fryer to bake. You can use the oven, but it takes longer to heat and more electricity. I don't know if it works in the microwave though.

Anyway, it is a great treat with few calories, high in fiber, and high in protein. I use sucralose as a sweetener, but you can use stevia, erythritol, xylitol, or any other zero-calorie sweetener. Same for protein powder, use any protein powder you want as long as it is chocolate flavored.

## Ingredients

- 200g bananas (2 medium)
- 200g avocado (1 big)
- 15g cacao
- 40g whey protein powder (or vegan)
- 1 egg
- 1g cinnamon powder
- zero calorie sweetener to taste (I used a few mg of sucralose)
- 1g salt
- 1g baking powder

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, mash the bananas and avocados until smooth
2. Add the cacao and mix
3. Add the protein powder and mix
4. Add the sweetener and mix
5. Add the egg and mix
6. Add the baking powder and mix
7. Line a small baking dish with parchment paper and pour the mixture there
8. Cook on the cake program in the air fryer (preheated at 200°C for 17 minutes)
9. Optionally, sprinkle some peanut powder on top
10. Enjoy!
