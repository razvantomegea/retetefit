This protein-rich tofu is perfect to start your day! The combination of tofu, broccoli, and rice offers a pleasant and satisfying texture, ideal for giving you the energy you need in the morning.

## Ingredients

- 1 tofu (approx. 200g)
- 1 bag of frozen broccoli (aprox. 500g)
- 50g basmati rice (or any rice you have)

## Instructions

1. Prepare the rice by boiling it with 300ml water (or as instructed on the bag)

2. Boil the broccoli for 6 minutes (or as instructed on the bag) in water with salt

3. Cut the tofu in small slices and pour soy sauce over by taste

4. Enjoy!
