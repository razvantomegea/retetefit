These protein pancakes are perfect to start your day! The combination of eggs, banana, and protein powder creates a fluffy and satisfying texture, perfect to give you the energy you need for the morning.

## Ingredients

- 2 large eggs
- 1 ripe banana (approx. 100g)
- 30g vanilla protein powder (or 2-3 egg whites)
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon coconut oil or butter for frying

### For topping (optional):

- Fresh berries
- Honey or maple syrup
- Nuts or ground almonds

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, mash the banana with a fork until creamy, almost like a paste.

2. Add the eggs and mix well until the mixture is uniform.

3. Incorporate the protein powder, cinnamon, and salt. Mix until no lumps remain.

4. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and grease it with a little coconut oil or butter.

5. Pour approximately 1/4 of the mixture for each pancake. Let it cook for 2-3 minutes on the first side, until bubbles appear on the surface.

6. Carefully flip the pancake and cook for another 1-2 minutes on the other side, until golden.

7. Repeat the process with the remaining mixture. You should get 6-8 small pancakes.

8. Serve immediately with your favorite toppings!

## Tips & Variations

- **No protein powder?** You can use 4-5 egg whites instead of powder. Add 1-2 tablespoons of oat flakes for consistency.

- **Vegan?** Try the vegan version with 2 tablespoons of almond butter, banana, and 30g almond or chickpea flour.

- **For more flavor:** Add vanilla extract, extra cinnamon, or chopped dark chocolate to the mixture.

- **Meal prep friendly:** You can prepare the mixture the night before and store it in the refrigerator. The pancakes keep well for 2-3 days in the refrigerator and can be quickly reheated in a pan or oven.

- **For a fluffier texture:** Add 1/2 teaspoon of baking powder to the mixture.

## Demo Ingredients (Chocolate Variation)

For a chocolate version, you can add:

- 1 tablespoon unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 tablespoon chopped dark chocolate (70% cocoa)
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon coconut sugar (optional, for sweetness)

## Demo Instructions (Oven Method)

For an alternative method without a pan:

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and prepare a baking sheet with parchment paper.

2. Follow steps 1-3 from the main instructions to prepare the mixture.

3. Pour the mixture into small pancake shapes on the parchment paper (approximately 2-3 cm in diameter).

4. Bake for 8-10 minutes, until the pancakes are fluffy and slightly golden on the edges.

5. Remove from the oven and let cool for 2-3 minutes before serving.

6. Perfect for meal prep - you can make a whole tray at once!
