Cream soups are a bit unusual in Romania, where people are used to traditional sour soups or stews rather than something that looks like baby food or pudding. Personally, I really enjoy this cream of mushroom soup — it is filling and tasty. Yes, it can also work as a meal for kids, why not. I say it is worth a try. Let us judge only after we taste it. Enjoy!

## Ingredients

- 4 bags mushroom cream soup mix (potatoes, celery root, parsnip, mushrooms)
- 1 onion
- 1 head of garlic
- 400 ml cooking cream
- 15 g Vegeta seasoning
- Black pepper to taste
- 1 L water
- Optional: parsley or dill

## Instructions

1. Peel and prep all the ingredients, then add them to the pressure cooker on the soup/stew with vegetables program (10 minutes).
2. Blend the entire mixture using an immersion blender or a regular blender.
3. Serve with croutons or toasted bread.
